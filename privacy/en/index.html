01 · SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Instagram links can I use?

Public post and Reel links are supported. Private, deleted, login- or age-restricted posts, and content restricted by Instagram may not be analyzed.

Why couldn't Pinpin find a place?

Pinpin looks for clues in public metadata, captions, location tags, and thumbnails. If these are unavailable, select up to 10 screenshots showing the place name or address and try again.

Does Pinpin access all my photos?

No. Pinpin loads only the images you choose through the iOS photo picker. Text recognition is performed on device with Apple Vision.

Are screenshots sent outside my device?

The original images are not sent to OpenAI. Text recognized on device is sent with the Instagram link and public post information to the OpenAI API to extract place names and map queries. Do not select screenshots containing sensitive information.

Where is my data stored?

Saved source links, extracted text, thumbnails, places, favorites, and notes are stored on your device by default. Pinpin currently provides no account, server sync, or automatic cross-device backup. Deleting the app deletes this on-device data.

Which maps are used?

Places in Korea can open in Naver Map or KakaoMap; places elsewhere open in Google Maps. A web page may open if the relevant app is unavailable.

When is the clipboard read?

Pinpin may use iOS pattern detection to offer import when the clipboard appears to contain an Instagram URL. The URL is read when you paste or start the suggested import.

How do I report a problem?

Email your device model, iOS and app versions, the step where the issue occurred, and the displayed error. Remove or obscure personal information from links and screenshots.

2.2 OpenAI API

To extract place names and map queries, Pinpin sends the Instagram URL, text from the public page, and on-device OCR text from thumbnails and selected screenshots to the OpenAI Responses API over encrypted connections. Original screenshot and thumbnail images are not sent. Requests use store: false. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, with legal or safety exceptions, and API data is not used to train models without explicit consent. See OpenAI API data controls.

2.3 On-device storage and retention

Source URLs, post identifiers and types, extracted/OCR text, thumbnails, saved places, favorites, and notes are stored locally with SwiftData until you delete the place or app. Deleting the final place from a post also deletes its source-post data. iOS backups are governed by your settings and Apple's policies. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to answer requests, resolve disputes, or satisfy law.
